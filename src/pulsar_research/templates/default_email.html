{# PULSAR email theme. Deliberately almost nothing. An earlier version wrapped the message in a rounded white card on a grey page in a serif face, which looked like a newsletter, and — because the body was a stack of styled blocks — pasted into a reply as a mess. A cold academic email should paste cleanly and read like mail, so the body here is plain paragraphs in the reader's own default size, with no background, no border and no table around it. The one deliberate piece of HTML is the footer card. It is self-contained, sits after the signature where it cannot interrupt the message, and carries the measurements that used to clutter the body. Its plaintext equivalent is `panels.card_lines`, rendered into the text alternative from the same values. Inline CSS only: Gmail and Outlook strip
